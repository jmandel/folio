## ED provider note

**History.** The patient presented this evening with several days of cough, feeling feverish and tired, and some shortness of breath with walking. There was no chest pain. Intake had been reduced over the same days. Allergies: NKDA.

**Home medications** (not yet reconciled at the time of this note): metformin 1000 mg twice daily with meals, dapagliflozin 10 mg daily, dulaglutide 0.5 mg subcutaneously weekly, losartan 100 mg daily, chlorthalidone 12.5 mg (half of a 25 mg tablet) daily, and atorvastatin 40 mg nightly.

**Exam.** Vitals at 19:40: BP 149/89, HR 99, RR 21, T 98.1 F, SpO2 95%. The patient was mildly tachypneic but spoke in full sentences. Lungs had coarse breath sounds with crackles at the base. Heart rhythm was regular. Mucous membranes were slightly dry. No leg swelling.

**Results.** The <ref id="cbc">CBC drawn at 20:08 showed white blood cells 16.2 (H), hemoglobin 12.3 and platelets 192</ref>. The basic metabolic panel from the same draw: sodium 133 (L), potassium 4.3, chloride 99, bicarbonate 22, anion gap 12, urea nitrogen 30 (H), creatinine 1.89 (H), eGFR 30 (L), glucose 152 (H). The raised white count together with the cough, fever history and crackles supports the diagnosis below. The kidney numbers and low sodium fit a patient who has been drinking and eating less than usual.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_pneumonia">Community-acquired pneumonia</ref>. Ceftriaxone 1 g IV every 24 hours was started at 21:04 and is running. Monitor oxygen saturation and work of breathing.
2. Elevated creatinine and low sodium with reduced intake. Normal saline at 75 mL/h was started at 20:24 and is still running. Home medications remain to be reconciled by the admitting team.

The patient is being admitted to the hospitalist service for IV antibiotics and monitoring of kidney function.
