## ED provider note

**Chief complaint:** nausea, fatigue and shortness of breath.

**History.** The patient is a man with type 2 diabetes and hypertension who presented feeling unwell, with nausea, marked tiredness and a sense of air hunger over the past day. He reports no fever and no chest pain. His home medications are <ref id="med_dapagliflozin">dapagliflozin 10 mg daily</ref>, metformin 1000 mg twice daily with meals, losartan 50 mg daily, hydrochlorothiazide 25 mg daily and atorvastatin 40 mg nightly. He has no known drug allergies.

**Exam.** At 16:01: BP 108/65, HR 86, RR 18, T 98.3 F, SpO2 96%. He looks tired and mildly dry. Lungs are clear. Abdomen is soft without focal tenderness.

**Results.** <ref id="bmp">Basic metabolic panel drawn at 16:24: sodium 134, potassium 4.8, chloride 96, bicarbonate 13, anion gap 25, urea nitrogen 30, creatinine 1.30, eGFR 44, glucose 240.</ref> Venous blood gas pH 7.13, pCO2 20. Beta-hydroxybutyrate 5.1.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_dka">Diabetic ketoacidosis, euglycemic</ref>: a wide anion gap metabolic acidosis with markedly elevated ketones despite a glucose of only 240. The pattern fits the SGLT2 inhibitor he takes. Insulin infusion per DKA protocol at 0.1 units/kg/h with dextrose-containing fluid, since glucose is not high.
2. Renal function: creatinine 1.30 with a high urea nitrogen, consistent with volume depletion. Fluids as above, repeat chemistries every 4 hours.
3. Admit to the hospitalist service for continued DKA management.
