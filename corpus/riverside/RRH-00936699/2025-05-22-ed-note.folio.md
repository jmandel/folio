## ED provider note

**Chief complaint:** nausea, vomiting and shortness of breath.

**History.** The patient has type 2 diabetes and reports several days of vomiting and very little to eat or drink. Today she became weak and breathless and came in. Her home list includes <ref id="med_empagliflozin">empagliflozin 10 mg daily</ref>, not yet reconciled. Also on the list and not yet reconciled: metformin 500 mg twice daily, glipizide 5 mg daily, insulin glargine 10 units nightly and atorvastatin 40 mg nightly. No known drug allergies.

**Exam.** At 17:26: BP 114/68, HR 106, RR 18, T 99.1 F, SpO2 94%. She appears dry and uncomfortable, with deep breathing. Abdomen soft with mild diffuse tenderness.

**Results.** Venous gas: pH 7.16, pCO2 26. Beta-hydroxybutyrate 5.7. <ref id="bmp">Basic metabolic panel at 17:46: sodium 130, potassium 4.6, chloride 97, bicarbonate 13, anion gap 20, urea nitrogen 29, creatinine 2.15, eGFR 34, glucose 155.</ref>

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_dka">Diabetic ketoacidosis, euglycemic</ref>: pH 7.16, bicarbonate 13, anion gap 20 and beta-hydroxybutyrate 5.7 with a glucose of 155. Insulin infusion per DKA protocol at 0.1 units/kg/h started 18:30, with D5 lactated Ringer's at 150 mL/h. Admit to the hospitalist service.
2. <ref id="dx_pancreatitis">R/O pancreatitis</ref> given the abdominal tenderness and vomiting; lipase was drawn with the 17:46 labs and is pending at the time of this note.
3. Hyponatremia and elevated creatinine, consistent with volume depletion; fluids as above.
