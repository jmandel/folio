## ED provider note

**Chief complaint:** weakness and abnormal labs.

**History.** The patient presented this afternoon feeling weak and dry after several days of back pain and poor oral intake. They denied chest pain, fainting or vomiting. Home medicines are lisinopril, which is now held, and rosuvastatin 20 mg nightly, which continues. No known drug allergies.

**Exam.** Vitals at 15:11: BP 110/66, HR 80, RR 18, T 99.1 F, SpO2 95%. Comfortable, mucous membranes slightly dry, heart regular, lungs clear, abdomen soft. No focal weakness.

**Results.** <ref id="bmp">Basic metabolic panel drawn 15:22: sodium 130, potassium 6.0, chloride 103, bicarbonate 22, anion gap 5, urea nitrogen 42, creatinine 1.87, eGFR 30, glucose 186.</ref>

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_hyperk">Hyperkalemia</ref>. I gave calcium gluconate 1 g IV at 15:45, completed 15:55, for membrane protection given the potassium of 6.0. Continue monitoring and recheck the potassium.
2. <ref id="dx_aki">Acute kidney injury</ref>, creatinine 1.87 with urea nitrogen 42. Normal saline 1000 mL bolus started 15:55, then 125 mL/h. Lisinopril is held.
3. Hyponatremia, sodium 130, mild. Expect improvement with fluids.
4. Admit to the hospitalist service for fluids, repeat labs and monitoring.
