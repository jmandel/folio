## Emergency department provider note

**History.** The patient was seen in the ED this evening with a cough and feeling unwell, with shortness of breath on exertion over the past several days. There was no chest pain and no vomiting. Allergy list: NKDA. The home medication list has been recorded but is not yet reconciled: metformin 1000 mg twice daily with meals, dapagliflozin 10 mg daily, glipizide 5 mg daily, insulin glargine 10 units nightly, losartan 100 mg daily, hydrochlorothiazide 25 mg daily, carvedilol 12.5 mg twice daily, spironolactone 25 mg daily, furosemide 40 mg daily and atorvastatin 40 mg nightly.

**Exam.** Vitals at 22:47: BP 110/66, HR 98, RR 23, T 98.5 F, SpO2 99%. The patient appeared mildly short of breath while speaking in full sentences. Breath sounds were diminished with crackles over the lung bases. Heart rhythm regular. Abdomen soft and nontender.

**Results.** On the 23:02 panel, <ref id="cbc">white blood cells 16.2 (H), hemoglobin 13.3 (L), platelets 267</ref>. Basic metabolic panel from the same draw: sodium 131 (L), potassium 4.7, chloride 102, bicarbonate 19 (L), anion gap 10, urea nitrogen 35 (H), creatinine 1.80 (H), eGFR 41 (L), glucose 173 (H).

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_pneumonia">Community-acquired pneumonia</ref>, based on the cough, breathlessness, lung exam and the elevated white count. Ceftriaxone 1 g IV every 24 hours was started at 00:11 and is running.
2. Low sodium, low bicarbonate and elevated urea nitrogen and creatinine on the 23:02 panel. Normal saline at 75 mL/h was started at 23:31 and is running. Repeat chemistry in the morning.
3. Home medications are not yet reconciled; this will be completed by the admitting team.

The patient will be admitted to the hospitalist service. Care was discussed with the patient, who agrees with admission.
