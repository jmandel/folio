## Emergency department provider note

**History.** The patient presented this evening with chest pain. The pain was not accompanied by any new neurologic symptoms, and there was no fainting. The allergy list reads NKDA. Home medications are on the chart (metformin, empagliflozin, losartan and amlodipine) and have not yet been reconciled.

**Exam.** Vitals at 20:40: BP 156/94, HR 110, RR 17, T 98.3 F, SpO2 96%. The patient is alert and speaking in full sentences. Heart rhythm is regular, lungs are clear, and the chest wall is non-tender.

**Results.** Basic metabolic panel drawn 6/7 at 21:03: sodium 140, potassium 4.4, chloride 103, bicarbonate 28, anion gap 9, urea nitrogen 20, creatinine 1.42 (H), eGFR 54 (L), glucose 108 (H). The creatinine and eGFR are mildly abnormal. <ref id="trop_1">Troponin I <0.01</ref> ng/mL on the 21:03 draw, and <ref id="trop_2">Troponin I <0.01</ref> ng/mL on the repeat drawn 6/8 at 00:03. The ECG showed no ischemic changes.

**Assessment and plan**

1. Chest pain: <ref id="dx_acs">Acute coronary syndrome ruled out</ref>: two troponins <0.01 three hours apart and an ECG without ischemic changes. The heart rate and blood pressure were elevated on arrival, and we will recheck both before the patient leaves the department.
2. Kidney function: creatinine 1.42 with eGFR 54 on today's panel; this should be rechecked with the primary care physician.
3. The patient was told what the results mean and was advised to return for any recurrent or worsening chest pain, shortness of breath, or fainting. Follow-up with the primary care physician in the next few days.
