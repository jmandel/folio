## ED provider note

**History.** The patient came in with chest pain that began earlier today, with some shortness of breath. They have type 2 diabetes and take metformin, losartan, carvedilol, spironolactone and furosemide at home; the home medication list has not yet been reconciled. The allergy list includes empagliflozin, which caused diabetic ketoacidosis.

**Exam.** Vitals at 14:11: BP 154/92, HR 97, RR 22, T 98.7 F, SpO2 95%. Uncomfortable but in no acute distress. Heart regular, lungs clear, chest wall non-tender, abdomen soft.

**Results.** The ECG showed no ischemic changes. Troponin I was <ref id="trop_1"><0.01 on the 14:25 draw</ref> and <ref id="trop_2"><0.01 on the 17:25 draw</ref>. Basic metabolic panel at 14:25: sodium 136, potassium 4.4, chloride 101, bicarbonate 24, anion gap 11, urea nitrogen 19, creatinine 0.73, eGFR 88, glucose 117.

**Assessment and plan**

1. Chest pain. <ref id="dx_acs">Acute coronary syndrome ruled out</ref>: two troponins <0.01 three hours apart and an ECG without ischemic changes. The metabolic panel is reassuring, with a normal bicarbonate and anion gap.
2. Blood pressure was elevated on arrival at 154/92. The patient was reassured about the work-up and advised to follow up with their primary care physician.
