## ED provider note

**History.** The patient came in with chest discomfort and some shortness of breath that began earlier today. They describe pressure in the chest without radiation. They have a history of diabetic ketoacidosis on empagliflozin, which is on the allergy list. Home medications (not yet reconciled) are metformin 1000 mg twice daily with meals, empagliflozin 10 mg daily, losartan 100 mg daily, carvedilol 12.5 mg twice daily, spironolactone 25 mg daily and furosemide 40 mg daily.

**Exam.** Vitals at 14:11: BP 154/92, HR 97, RR 22, T 98.7 F, SpO2 95%. Uncomfortable but in no distress, lungs clear, regular rhythm, chest wall non-tender.

**Results.** Basic metabolic panel at 14:25: sodium 136, potassium 4.4, chloride 101, bicarbonate 24, anion gap 11, urea nitrogen 19, creatinine 0.73, eGFR 88, glucose 117. The first troponin, <ref id="trop_1">troponin I <0.01</ref> at 14:25, and the second, <ref id="trop_2">troponin I <0.01</ref> at 17:25, were both undetectable. The ECG showed no ischemic changes.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_acs">Chest pain, acute coronary syndrome ruled out.</ref> Acute coronary syndrome ruled out: two troponins <0.01 three hours apart and an ECG without ischemic changes.
2. Blood pressure was elevated on arrival at 154/92; recheck before discharge.
3. Follow up with the primary care physician, and return for worsening pain, shortness of breath or fainting.
