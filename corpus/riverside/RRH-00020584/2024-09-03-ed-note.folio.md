## ED provider note

**History.** The patient presented with several days of feeling unwell, with nausea, abdominal discomfort and fatigue, and was breathing fast on arrival. They take empagliflozin at home along with metformin, losartan, carvedilol, spironolactone and furosemide. The home medication list has not yet been reconciled. Allergy list: NKDA.

**Exam.** Vitals at 18:54: BP 107/64, HR 85, RR 23, T 98.7 F, SpO2 97%. Tired-appearing, with deep, rapid respirations. Mucous membranes are dry. Abdomen is soft with mild diffuse tenderness and no guarding.

**Results.** Venous blood gas drawn at 19:11: pH 7.09, pCO2 29. Beta-hydroxybutyrate 4.4 mmol/L on the same draw. <ref id="bmp">Basic metabolic panel at 19:11: sodium 128, potassium 5.6, chloride 92, bicarbonate 14, anion gap 22, urea nitrogen 39, creatinine 0.85, eGFR 75, glucose 186</ref>.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_dka">Diabetic ketoacidosis, euglycemic</ref>. The glucose is near normal but the patient has a significant anion gap acidosis with a markedly elevated ketone level, in a patient who takes <ref id="med_empagliflozin">empagliflozin 10 mg daily</ref>. An insulin infusion per DKA protocol was started at 19:58 and is running. Potassium is high at 5.6 and will be rechecked with the serial panels.
2. <ref id="dx_pancreatitis">R/O pancreatitis</ref> given the abdominal discomfort. Lipase is pending.
3. Admit to the hospitalist service for continued DKA management.
