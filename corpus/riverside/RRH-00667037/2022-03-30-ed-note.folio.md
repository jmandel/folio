## ED provider note

**Chief complaint.** Cough and feeling unwell.

**History.** The patient presented to the emergency department this afternoon feeling unwell, with cough and a sense of being run down. They take losartan, chlorthalidone and amlodipine at home; these have not yet been reconciled in the ED. No known drug allergies.

**Exam.** Vitals at 14:11: BP 116/70, HR 104, RR 16, temperature 98.4 F, SpO2 99%. The patient is mildly tachycardic but breathing comfortably on room air readings and speaking in full sentences.

**Results.** The <ref id="cbc">complete blood count drawn at 14:31 shows a white blood cell count of 17.3 (high), hemoglobin 12.4 (low) and platelets 261</ref>. The basic metabolic panel from the same draw shows sodium 137, potassium 4.5, chloride 98, bicarbonate 21 (low), anion gap 18 (high), urea nitrogen 46 (high), creatinine 2.14 (high), eGFR 34 (low) and glucose 127 (high).

**ED course.** Intravenous normal saline was started at 14:55 at 75 mL/h and is still running. Ceftriaxone 1 g IV every 24 hours was started at 15:35 and is still running.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_pneumonia">Community-acquired pneumonia</ref>, with a high white count and tachycardia. Ceftriaxone has been started as above.
2. Elevated creatinine with a raised anion gap and low bicarbonate. Saline is running; the hospitalist service will follow the kidney function.
3. The patient is to be admitted to the hospitalist service for further care.
