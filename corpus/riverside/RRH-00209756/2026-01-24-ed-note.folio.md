## ED provider note

**Chief complaint.** Cough and feeling unwell.

**History.** The patient was seen this evening with a cough and low-grade fever. Home medications are metformin 500 mg twice daily, glipizide 5 mg daily, losartan 50 mg daily and atorvastatin 40 mg nightly; they have not been reconciled in the ED. No known drug allergies.

**Exam.** Vitals at 21:11: BP 138/83, HR 88, RR 16, T 99.1 F, SpO2 95%. Alert, no distress. Crackles at the base of the lungs; heart regular; abdomen soft.

**Results.** <ref id="cbc">CBC at 21:39: white blood cells 15.8, hemoglobin 13.4, platelets 217</ref>. BMP at 21:39: sodium 129, potassium 4.8, chloride 103, bicarbonate 22, anion gap 4, urea nitrogen 48, creatinine 2.12, eGFR 27, glucose 153.

**ED course.** Normal saline was started at 21:55 at 75 mL/h and is still running. Ceftriaxone 1 g IV every 24 hours was started at 22:35.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_pneumonia">Community-acquired pneumonia</ref>, with the raised white count and the chest findings. Continue ceftriaxone and admit to the hospitalist service.
2. Kidney function and sodium: creatinine 2.12, urea nitrogen 48 and sodium 129. Continue gentle IV saline and recheck labs in the morning.
3. Admit to medicine; the hospitalist will decide on the home medications.
