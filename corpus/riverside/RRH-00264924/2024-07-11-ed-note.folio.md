## ED provider note

**Chief complaint:** abnormal labs, weakness, and several days of back pain.

**History.** The patient presented this afternoon feeling run down after several days of back pain and reduced eating and drinking. No chest pain, no fainting, no vomiting. Home medications are lisinopril 10 mg daily, amlodipine 5 mg daily, atorvastatin 40 mg nightly and levothyroxine 75 mcg daily. No known drug allergies.

**Exam.** At 16:40: BP 115/69, HR 101, RR 18, temperature 98.2 F, SpO2 96%. Mildly dry mucous membranes. Heart regular and slightly fast, lungs clear, abdomen soft and non-tender.

**Results.** <ref id="bmp">Basic metabolic panel drawn at 17:00: sodium 136, potassium 6.7 (critical), chloride 103, bicarbonate 19, anion gap 14, urea nitrogen 48, creatinine 2.02, eGFR 39, glucose 163.</ref>

**ED course.** With the critical potassium, calcium gluconate 1 g IV was given from 17:14 to 17:24 for membrane protection. Normal saline 1000 mL bolus was started at 17:24, followed by 125 mL/h, and is still running. The patient tolerated both without difficulty.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_hyperk">Hyperkalemia</ref>, potassium 6.7, treated with IV calcium as above.
2. <ref id="dx_aki">Acute kidney injury</ref>, creatinine 2.02 with urea nitrogen 48, being treated with IV fluids.
3. The patient will be admitted to the hospitalist service for continued fluids and repeat labs.
