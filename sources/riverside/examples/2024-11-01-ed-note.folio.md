## ED provider note

**Arrived** 11/01/2024 21:40. **Chief complaint:** vomiting, short of breath.

**History.** 59-year-old woman with type 2 diabetes, hypertension and chronic kidney disease. Three
days of nausea, vomiting and loose stools; since this afternoon she feels short of breath and weak.
Vague epigastric discomfort, no radiation. Denies chest pain, fever, black or bloody stools.

**Exam.** Ill-appearing, dry mucous membranes. Deep, regular respirations (Kussmaul). Tachycardic,
regular. Mild epigastric tenderness without guarding.

**Results.** <ref id="bmp">Glucose only 182, but bicarbonate 11 and anion gap 24</ref>.
<ref id="vbg">Venous pH 7.21</ref>. <ref id="bhb">Beta-hydroxybutyrate 6.2</ref>. Sodium 129, potassium
5.0, creatinine 1.41. <ref id="trop">Troponin I <0.01</ref>; ECG sinus tachycardia at 116 without
ischemic changes. No evidence of ACS. Lipase pending at the time of this note.

**Assessment.** <ref id="dx_dka">Diabetic ketoacidosis with near-normal glucose</ref>. She takes
<ref id="med_empa">empagliflozin</ref>, and euglycemic DKA from an SGLT2 inhibitor is the leading
explanation. <ref id="dx_panc">R/O pancreatitis</ref> given epigastric pain; lipase sent.
<ref id="dx_na">Hyponatremia</ref>, likely from volume loss.

**Plan.** <ref id="iv_lr">1 L lactated Ringer's bolus</ref>, then <ref id="iv_insulin">insulin infusion
per DKA protocol</ref> started 23:05 with dextrose-containing fluids. Home medications listed, not
reconciled. Admitted to Hospital Medicine, step-down.
