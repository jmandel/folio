## ED provider note

**History.** The patient presented this evening with a cough and feeling unwell, with low-grade fever and some shortness of breath on exertion. There was no chest pain. No allergies to medications are on file (NKDA). The home medication list has not yet been reconciled.

**Exam.** Vitals at 22:40: BP 116/70, HR 103, RR 20, T 99 F, SpO2 96%. The patient is tired-appearing but conversant and in no acute distress. Breath sounds are diminished with crackles at the base on the examined side. Heart is regular and mildly tachycardic. Abdomen is soft and nontender.

**Results.** Labs on the 22:53 panel: <ref id="cbc">White blood cells 17.4 10*3/uL (H), Hemoglobin 13.6 g/dL, Platelets 206 10*3/uL</ref>. Basic metabolic panel: sodium 133 (L), potassium 4.4, chloride 96 (L), bicarbonate 22, anion gap 15, urea nitrogen 34 (H), creatinine 2.48 (H), eGFR 25 (L), glucose 190 (H).

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_pneumonia">Community-acquired pneumonia</ref>, supported by the symptoms, the focal chest findings and the elevated white count. Ceftriaxone 1 g IV every 24 hours was started at 00:04 and is running.
2. Elevated creatinine of 2.48 with urea nitrogen 34. Normal saline at 75 mL/h was started at 23:24 and is still running. Kidney function will be rechecked with the next panel.
3. Disposition: admit to the hospitalist service for further treatment and monitoring. The patient agrees with the plan.
