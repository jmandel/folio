## Admission history and physical

**Reason for admission:** euglycemic diabetic ketoacidosis.

**History of present illness.** The patient came to the ED last night with nausea, deep breathing and weakness. <ref id="precipitant">The ketoacidosis was precipitated by a weekend of heavy alcohol use with little food.</ref> Abdominal discomfort was present in the ED and prompted a check for pancreatitis. Allergy list: NKDA.

**Medications at home.** Dapagliflozin, metformin, losartan, furosemide, semaglutide, carvedilol, spironolactone and rosuvastatin; their plans are in the problem list below.

**Exam.** Vitals at 03:25: BP 107/64, HR 105, RR 20, T 99.1 F, SpO2 96%. Dry mucous membranes, mildly tachycardic, comfortable at rest. Abdomen soft.

**Results.** The 00:08 panel showed sodium 131, potassium 5.2, chloride 98, bicarbonate 8, anion gap 25, urea nitrogen 25, creatinine 1.11, eGFR 61 and glucose 227. Lipase 24.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_dka">Euglycemic DKA, SGLT2 inhibitor-associated</ref>. Continue the insulin infusion per DKA protocol (0.1 units/kg/h, started 00:58) with D5 lactated Ringer's at 150 mL/h, since glucose is not high. <ref id="med_dapagliflozin">Hold dapagliflozin.</ref>
2. <ref id="dx_pancreatitis">Acute pancreatitis ruled out</ref>: lipase 24.
3. Metformin: <ref id="med_metformin">hold while acidotic and NPO</ref>; resume when able.
4. Volume depletion with potassium 5.2: <ref id="med_losartan">hold losartan</ref>, and resume when eating. <ref id="med_furosemide">Hold furosemide</ref> for volume depletion, to resume when able.
5. Continue semaglutide, carvedilol, spironolactone and rosuvastatin.
