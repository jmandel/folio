## ED provider note

**History.** The patient presented with chest pain that brought them to the ED today. Pain was described as a pressure-like discomfort in the chest, not clearly related to exertion, with some associated fast heartbeat. No fainting. No known drug allergies. Home medications on the list are metformin 1000 mg twice daily, lisinopril 20 mg daily and rosuvastatin 20 mg nightly; these have not yet been reconciled.

**Exam.** Vitals at 16:11: BP 142/85, HR 110, RR 19, T 98.2 F, SpO2 96%. Comfortable at rest, speaking full sentences. Heart rhythm regular and tachycardic, lungs clear, chest wall nontender, no leg swelling.

**Results.** <ref id="trop_1">Troponin I <0.01 ng/mL</ref> on the 16:21 panel and <ref id="trop_2">Troponin I <0.01 ng/mL</ref> on the repeat at 19:21. The ECG showed no ischemic changes. Basic metabolic panel drawn 16:21: sodium 139, potassium 4.4, chloride 100, bicarbonate 23, anion gap 16, urea nitrogen 19, creatinine 1.08 (H), eGFR 55 (L), glucose 111 (H).

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_acs">Acute coronary syndrome ruled out</ref>: two troponins <0.01 three hours apart and an ECG without ischemic changes. The heart rate settled with rest and observation in the department, and the pain did not recur.
2. Mildly reduced eGFR with creatinine 1.08; the rest of the metabolic panel is unremarkable. Outpatient follow-up of kidney function with the primary care physician.
3. Return precautions reviewed: recurrent or worsening chest pain, shortness of breath, or fainting should prompt immediate return. Follow up with primary care within the week.
