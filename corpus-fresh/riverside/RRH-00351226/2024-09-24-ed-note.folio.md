## ED provider note

**History.** The patient presented with several days of feeling unwell, nausea, and fast, deep breathing. No known drug allergies. They take empagliflozin, metformin, lisinopril, rosuvastatin and insulin glargine 10 units nightly at home.

**Exam.** Vitals at 14:54: BP 111/67, HR 108, RR 24, T 98.1 F, SpO2 96%. Tachypneic with deep respirations, dry mucous membranes, abdomen soft without focal tenderness.

**Results.** Venous blood gas drawn 15:20: pH 7.14 (LL), pCO2 22 (L). Beta-hydroxybutyrate 6.7 mmol/L on the same draw. <ref id="bmp">Basic metabolic panel: sodium 130 mmol/L (L), potassium 5.3 (H), chloride 98, bicarbonate 13 (L), anion gap 19 (H), urea nitrogen 37 (H), creatinine 1.30 (H), eGFR 44 (L), glucose 215 (H)</ref>.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_dka">Euglycemic DKA, SGLT2 inhibitor-associated</ref>: glucose is only modestly raised, but pH 7.14, bicarbonate 13 and beta-hydroxybutyrate 6.7 establish ketoacidosis. Insulin infusion per DKA protocol at 0.1 units/kg/h, started 15:58. <ref id="med_empagliflozin">Empagliflozin is held.</ref>
2. Metformin is held. Lisinopril is held given the potassium and creatinine. Glargine and rosuvastatin continue.
3. Admit to the hospitalist service.
