## Discharge summary

**Diagnoses.** <ref id="dx_dka">Euglycemic DKA, SGLT2 inhibitor-associated, resolved.</ref> <ref id="dx_pancreatitis">Acute pancreatitis was ruled out</ref> during the workup.

**Hospital course.** <ref id="precipitant">The ketoacidosis was brought on by elective knee replacement surgery, with a long pre-operative fast while the SGLT2 inhibitor was continued.</ref> She was treated with an insulin infusion and fluids and improved steadily. Her nausea resolved and she is eating. She has no known drug allergies.

**Discharge exam and results.** Vitals at 09:45: BP 118/71, HR 106, RR 16, T 99 F, SpO2 99%. The final panel, drawn 4/16 at 06:28, showed sodium 132, potassium 4.2, chloride 101, bicarbonate 19, anion gap 12, urea nitrogen 22, creatinine 1.33, eGFR 60 and glucose 131.

**Discharge medications**

- <ref id="med_empagliflozin">Empagliflozin 10 mg daily: do not resume until reviewed by her PCP.</ref>
- <ref id="med_metformin">Metformin 1000 mg twice daily with meals: continue.</ref>
- <ref id="med_losartan">Losartan 100 mg daily: continue.</ref>
- <ref id="med_hydrochlorothiazide">Hydrochlorothiazide 25 mg daily: continue.</ref>
- Semaglutide 0.5 mg SubQ weekly: continue.

**Follow-up.** Primary care at Northgate Family Medicine within 1 week, to decide whether to restart empagliflozin and to recheck a basic metabolic panel. She should return for vomiting, abdominal pain, fast breathing or inability to eat or drink.
