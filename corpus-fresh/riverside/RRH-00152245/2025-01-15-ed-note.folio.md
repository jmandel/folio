## ED provider note

**Chief complaint.** Diarrhea, weakness and lightheadedness.

**History.** The patient has type 2 diabetes and hypertension and presented after several days of diarrhea with poor oral intake. She feels weak and dizzy on standing. Allergy list: lisinopril (cough); dapagliflozin (diabetic ketoacidosis). Her home metformin and losartan have not yet been reconciled.

**Exam.** BP 144/86, HR 83, RR 21, T 98.2 F, SpO2 97% at 15:40. She is alert and appears mildly dehydrated, with dry mucous membranes. Abdomen is soft and nontender.

**Results.** The <ref id="bmp">16:02 panel shows sodium 135, potassium 6.2, chloride 99, bicarbonate 21, anion gap 15, urea nitrogen 32, creatinine 2.46, eGFR 19 and glucose 181</ref>.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_hyperk">Hyperkalemia</ref> with potassium 6.2. Calcium gluconate 1 g IV was given at 16:14 to protect the heart.
2. <ref id="dx_aki">Acute kidney injury</ref> with creatinine 2.46 and eGFR 19, likely from volume loss. Normal saline 1000 mL bolus started at 16:24, then 125 mL/h.
3. Admit to the hospitalist service for fluids and repeat chemistries.
