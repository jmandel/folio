## ED provider note

**Chief complaint.** Cough, fever and shortness of breath.

**History.** The patient reports several days of productive cough, fever and chills, with breathlessness on exertion and poor oral intake. No chest pain. Allergy: lisinopril (cough). Home medications reported but not yet reconciled: metformin, empagliflozin, losartan, rosuvastatin and one other daily medication.

**Exam.** Vitals at 18:18: BP 113/68, HR 103, RR 21, T 98.7 F, SpO2 97%. Mildly tachycardic and breathing a little fast. Crackles at the right lung base. Mucous membranes dry.

**Results.** <ref id="cbc">Complete blood count drawn 18:37: white blood cells 14.9, hemoglobin 13.4, platelets 335.</ref> Basic metabolic panel at the same draw: sodium 133, potassium 4.3, chloride 102, bicarbonate 19, anion gap 12, urea nitrogen 54, creatinine 1.91, eGFR 30, glucose 171.

**Assessment and plan.** The picture of fever, cough, focal crackles and an elevated white count is consistent with <ref id="dx_pneumonia">community-acquired pneumonia</ref>. Normal saline at 75 mL/h was started at 19:02 for dehydration, and ceftriaxone 1 g IV every 24 hours was started at 19:42. The patient is stable and is admitted to the hospitalist service.
