## Admission history and physical

**Reason for admission:** euglycemic diabetic ketoacidosis.

**History of present illness.** The patient presented to the ED this afternoon feeling weak, nauseated and short of breath, with a dry mouth and poor appetite. <ref id="precipitant">The ketoacidosis was precipitated by a weekend of heavy alcohol use with little food.</ref> At home the patient takes an SGLT2 inhibitor along with the other medications listed below. There are no known drug allergies.

**Exam.** Vitals at 20:29: BP 110/66, HR 106, RR 19, T 99.1 F, SpO2 94%. The patient looks dry, with tacky mucous membranes and mildly fast, deep breathing. Abdomen is soft.

**Results.** The 16:53 basic metabolic panel shows sodium 132, potassium 4.7, chloride 96, bicarbonate 13, anion gap 23, urea nitrogen 39, creatinine 1.53 (eGFR 49) and glucose 232. The glucose is only modestly raised for the degree of acidosis.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_dka">Euglycemic DKA, SGLT2 inhibitor-associated</ref>. The insulin infusion per DKA protocol (0.1 units/kg/h) and D5 lactated Ringer's at 150 mL/h were started at 17:30 and are still running. Continue both and recheck the panel through the night. The offending agent is stopped: <ref id="med_dapagliflozin">hold dapagliflozin 10 mg daily.</ref>
2. Diabetes: <ref id="med_metformin">hold metformin</ref> while acidotic and NPO. Semaglutide 0.5 mg weekly continues.
3. Volume depletion: <ref id="med_hydrochlorothiazide">hold hydrochlorothiazide</ref> and <ref id="med_furosemide">hold furosemide</ref> for volume depletion; resume when able.
4. Blood pressure and heart: <ref id="med_lisinopril">continue lisinopril 20 mg daily</ref>. Continue carvedilol 12.5 mg twice daily and spironolactone 25 mg daily.
5. Continue atorvastatin 20 mg nightly.
