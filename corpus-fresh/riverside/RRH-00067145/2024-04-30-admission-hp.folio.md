## Admission history and physical

**Reason for admission:** euglycemic diabetic ketoacidosis.

**History of present illness.** The patient was seen in the ED this evening with nausea, malaise and fast breathing after a knee replacement. Labs drawn at 18:45 showed a bicarbonate of 14 with an anion gap of 20 and a glucose of only 192. <ref id="precipitant">The ketoacidosis was precipitated by elective knee replacement surgery with prolonged pre-operative fasting while the SGLT2 inhibitor was continued.</ref> She has no known drug allergies. An insulin infusion and D5 lactated Ringer's at 150 mL/h were started at 19:30 and are still running.

**Home medications.** Empagliflozin 10 mg daily, metformin 500 mg twice daily, losartan 50 mg daily, furosemide 40 mg daily, carvedilol 12.5 mg twice daily, spironolactone 25 mg daily and atorvastatin 20 mg nightly.

**Exam.** At 23:20: BP 127/76, HR 93, RR 19, T 98.3 F, SpO2 97%. She is comfortable and breathing easily on room air, with a soft abdomen.

**Results.** Sodium 132, potassium 5.1, chloride 98, bicarbonate 14, anion gap 20, BUN 28, creatinine 1.14, eGFR 64, glucose 192. Lipase 30.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_dka">Euglycemic DKA, SGLT2 inhibitor-associated</ref>. Continue the insulin infusion per DKA protocol with dextrose-containing fluids, since glucose is near normal. <ref id="med_empagliflozin">Hold empagliflozin.</ref>
2. <ref id="dx_pancreatitis">Acute pancreatitis ruled out</ref>: lipase 30.
3. Volume depletion. <ref id="med_furosemide">Hold furosemide</ref> for volume depletion and resume when able.
4. Diabetes: <ref id="med_metformin">hold metformin</ref> while acidotic and NPO.
5. Hypertension: <ref id="med_losartan">hold losartan</ref> for volume depletion and potassium of 5.1; resume when she is eating.
6. Continue carvedilol, spironolactone and atorvastatin.
