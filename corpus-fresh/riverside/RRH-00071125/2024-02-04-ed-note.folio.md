## ED provider note

**History.** The patient presented this evening feeling weak and washed out after several days of diarrhea with poor intake. Home medications have not yet been reconciled in the ED record. Allergy list: lisinopril (cough).

**Exam.** Vitals at 20:26: BP 116/70, HR 81, RR 18, T 98.5 F, SpO2 99%. Dry mouth, mildly tired appearing, lungs clear, abdomen soft and non-tender.

**Results.** The basic metabolic panel drawn at 20:48 is notable. <ref id="bmp">Basic metabolic panel: sodium 136, potassium 6.5 (HH), chloride 98, bicarbonate 19 (L), anion gap 19 (H), urea nitrogen 52 (H), creatinine 2.36 (H), eGFR 23 (L), glucose 162 (H)</ref>.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_hyperk">Hyperkalemia</ref> with a potassium of 6.5. Calcium gluconate 1 g IV was given from 21:00 to 21:10 for membrane protection, and the patient is on a cardiac monitor.
2. <ref id="dx_aki">Acute kidney injury</ref>, with creatinine 2.36 and urea nitrogen 52. Normal saline 1000 mL bolus was started at 21:10, followed by 125 mL/h, and it is still running.
3. Admit to the hospitalist service for further treatment, repeat chemistry, and monitoring of urine output.
