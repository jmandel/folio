## ED provider note

**History.** The patient came to the emergency department with chest pain that brought them in this afternoon. The pain was not pleasant but was not crushing, and there was no fainting. They were comfortable and talkative at the bedside and appeared well. Home medications have not yet been reconciled in the ED record; the allergy list shows NKDA.

**Exam.** Vitals at 18:18: BP 157/94, HR 97, RR 16, T 98.3 F, SpO2 98%. Alert and in no distress. Lungs clear, heart regular without murmur. Chest wall without reproducible tenderness. No leg swelling.

**Results.** ECG showed no ischemic changes. The first troponin was drawn at 18:38: <ref id="trop_1">Troponin I <0.01 ng/mL</ref>. The repeat was drawn at 21:38: <ref id="trop_2">Troponin I <0.01 ng/mL</ref>. Basic metabolic panel drawn at 18:38: sodium 138, potassium 4.0, chloride 99, bicarbonate 26, anion gap 13, urea nitrogen 23, creatinine 0.98, eGFR 69, glucose 142 (H).

**Assessment and plan**

1. Chest pain. <ref id="dx_acs">Acute coronary syndrome ruled out</ref>: two troponins <0.01 three hours apart and an ECG without ischemic changes. With a normal electrolyte and kidney panel and a stable exam, there is no finding here that needs admission.
2. Blood pressure was elevated on arrival at 157/94 while the patient was in pain and anxious; recommend a recheck with their primary care physician.
3. Disposition: discharge home with instructions to return for recurrent or worsening chest pain, shortness of breath, or fainting, and to see their primary care physician within the week.
