## ED provider note

**Chief complaint.** Nausea, weakness and rapid breathing.

**History.** The patient came in feeling unwell with nausea, poor appetite and deep, fast breathing. No known drug allergies (NKDA). Home medications are listed in the chart and have not yet been reconciled; they include <ref id="med_empagliflozin">empagliflozin 10 mg daily</ref>, as well as metformin, losartan, hydrochlorothiazide, carvedilol, spironolactone, furosemide and atorvastatin.

**Exam.** Vitals at 15:47: BP 102/61, HR 91, RR 24, T 98.3 F, SpO2 99%. Ill-appearing but alert, with dry mucous membranes and deep respirations. Abdomen soft.

**Results.** Venous blood gas at 16:13: pH 7.16, pCO2 20. Beta-hydroxybutyrate 6.5 mmol/L. <ref id="bmp">Basic metabolic panel at 16:13: sodium 134, potassium 5.5, chloride 98, bicarbonate 10, anion gap 26, urea nitrogen 32, creatinine 1.51, eGFR 52, glucose 236.</ref>

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_dka">Diabetic ketoacidosis, euglycemic</ref>: severe acidosis with a wide anion gap, markedly elevated ketones and a glucose of only 236. An insulin infusion per DKA protocol at 0.1 units/kg/h was started at 16:51 and is running, with IV fluids.
2. Hyperkalemia, potassium 5.5, and elevated creatinine, consistent with volume depletion. Monitor with repeat chemistries.
3. Admit to the hospitalist service for continued DKA management.
