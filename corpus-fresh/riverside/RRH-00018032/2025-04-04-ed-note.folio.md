## ED provider note

**History.** The patient came to the ED with cough and shortness of breath. No known drug allergies. Home medications include metformin, dapagliflozin, glipizide, insulin glargine, lisinopril, chlorthalidone and amlodipine.

**Exam.** At 17:47: BP 134/80, HR 86, RR 19, T 98.7 F, SpO2 94%. Appears mildly short of breath with talking but is not in distress.

**Results.** On the 18:15 panel, <ref id="cbc">white blood cells 16.2 (high), hemoglobin 13.1, platelets 326</ref>. Chemistry from the same draw: sodium 134 (low), potassium 4.3, chloride 104, bicarbonate 23, anion gap 7, urea nitrogen 60, creatinine 1.49, eGFR 36 and glucose 185.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_pneumonia">Community-acquired pneumonia</ref>, active. Ceftriaxone 1 g IV every 24 hours was started at 19:11 and is running. Normal saline at 75 mL/h was started at 18:31 and continues, given the urea nitrogen of 60 and creatinine of 1.49.
2. Metformin and dapagliflozin are held. Glipizide, insulin glargine 10 units nightly, lisinopril, chlorthalidone and amlodipine are continued as at home.
3. Admit to the hospitalist service for further care.
