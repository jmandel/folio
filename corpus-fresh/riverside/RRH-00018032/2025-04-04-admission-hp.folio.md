## Admission history and physical

**Reason for admission:** pneumonia acquired in the community.

**History.** The patient was seen in the ED this afternoon with cough and shortness of breath and was brought in for treatment and observation. Allergy list: no known drug allergies. Home medications are glipizide 5 mg daily, insulin glargine 10 units nightly, lisinopril 40 mg daily, chlorthalidone 12.5 mg (half of a 25 mg tablet) daily, amlodipine 5 mg daily, metformin 1000 mg twice daily with meals and dapagliflozin 10 mg daily.

**Exam.** At 21:21: BP 120/72, HR 104, RR 24, T 98.3 F, SpO2 94%. Mildly tachypneic and mildly tachycardic but speaking in full sentences and comfortable at rest.

**Results.** The 18:15 panel showed sodium 134 (low), potassium 4.3, bicarbonate 23, anion gap 7, urea nitrogen 60, creatinine 1.49, eGFR 36 and glucose 185. Normal saline has been running at 75 mL/h since 18:31.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_pneumonia">Community-acquired pneumonia, active</ref>. Continue treatment and monitor oxygen saturation and respiratory rate. Continue the saline at 75 mL/h given the elevated urea nitrogen and creatinine, and recheck a basic panel in the morning.
2. <ref id="med_metformin">Hold metformin</ref> during the acute illness; resume when able.
3. <ref id="med_dapagliflozin">Hold dapagliflozin</ref> while she is not eating; resume when able.
4. Continue glipizide, insulin glargine, lisinopril, chlorthalidone and amlodipine as at home.
