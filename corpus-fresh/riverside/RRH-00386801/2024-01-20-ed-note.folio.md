## Emergency department provider note

**History.** The patient is an adult with type 2 diabetes who came to the ED after several days of feeling weak and run down, with reduced urine output and poor intake of fluids. Home medications include metformin 500 mg twice daily with meals and atorvastatin 20 mg nightly; the medication list has not been reconciled in the ED. Allergy list: empagliflozin (diabetic ketoacidosis).

**Exam.** Vitals at 19:26: BP 119/71, HR 90, RR 16, temperature 98.2 F, SpO2 98%. Alert and conversant, mildly dry mucous membranes, no distress.

**Results.** <ref id="bmp">Basic metabolic panel drawn 19:50: sodium 135 (L), potassium 6.5 (HH), chloride 100, bicarbonate 18 (L), anion gap 17 (H), urea nitrogen 51 (H), creatinine 1.89 (H), eGFR 29 (L), glucose 173 (H).</ref>

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_hyperk">Hyperkalemia</ref>, potassium 6.5. Calcium gluconate 1 g IV was given 20:00 to 20:10 to protect the heart. Repeat potassium to follow.
2. <ref id="dx_aki">Acute kidney injury</ref>, creatinine 1.89 with eGFR 29 and a urea nitrogen of 51, consistent with volume depletion. Started a 1000 mL normal saline bolus at 20:10, followed by 125 mL/h.
3. The patient will be admitted to the hospitalist service for monitoring and further treatment. The patient was updated on the plan and agrees.
