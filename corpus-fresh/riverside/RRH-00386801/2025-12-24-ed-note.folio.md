## Emergency department provider note

**History.** The patient is an adult with type 2 diabetes who came to the ED on the evening of 12/24 with chest pain. The pain was not clearly exertional, and there was no fainting. Home medications include metformin 500 mg twice daily with meals and atorvastatin 20 mg nightly; the medication list has not been reconciled in the ED. Allergy list: empagliflozin (diabetic ketoacidosis).

**Exam.** Vitals at 23:11: BP 156/94, HR 99, RR 20, temperature 98.1 F, SpO2 98%. Comfortable, speaking in full sentences, lungs clear, heart regular, chest wall without a clear reproducible source.

**Results.** Basic metabolic panel drawn 23:35: sodium 138, potassium 4.1, chloride 105, bicarbonate 24, anion gap 9, urea nitrogen 21, creatinine 0.76, eGFR 86, glucose 148 (H). Serial troponins: <ref id="trop_1">Troponin I <0.01 at 23:35</ref> and <ref id="trop_2">troponin I <0.01 at 02:35</ref>. The ECG shows no ischemic changes.

**Assessment and plan**

1. Final diagnosis: <ref id="dx_acs">Chest pain, acute coronary syndrome ruled out</ref>. Acute coronary syndrome ruled out: two troponins <0.01 three hours apart and an ECG without ischemic changes. The pain settled during the observation period.
2. Blood pressure was elevated on arrival at 156/94 and should be rechecked at follow-up with the primary care physician.
3. The patient is stable for discharge home with return precautions for recurrent or worsening chest pain, shortness of breath or fainting.
