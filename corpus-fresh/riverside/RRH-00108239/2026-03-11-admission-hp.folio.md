## Admission history and physical

**Reason for admission:** euglycemic diabetic ketoacidosis.

**History of present illness.** The patient presented to the ED with malaise, nausea and fast breathing and was found to be acidotic with a glucose of only 159. No clear precipitant was identified: no infection, no missed meals reported, no alcohol, no recent surgery. The patient takes <ref id="med_empagliflozin">empagliflozin 10 mg daily</ref>, which is held.

**Allergies:** lisinopril (cough).

**Exam.** At 01:20: BP 120/72, HR 92, RR 23, T 98.4 F, SpO2 94%. Dry mucous membranes, deep respirations, abdomen soft.

**Results.** Sodium 132, potassium 4.9, chloride 100, bicarbonate 14, anion gap 18, urea nitrogen 35, creatinine 1.14, eGFR 58, glucose 159 on the 20:55 panel. Lipase 53.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_dka">Euglycemic DKA, SGLT2 inhibitor-associated</ref>. Continue the insulin infusion per DKA protocol (started 21:44) with D5 lactated Ringer's at 150 mL/h, since glucose is near normal. Empagliflozin remains held.
2. <ref id="dx_pancreatitis">Acute pancreatitis ruled out</ref>: lipase 53, and the abdomen is soft.
3. Volume depletion: <ref id="med_furosemide">hold furosemide 40 mg daily</ref> for volume depletion; resume when able.
4. <ref id="med_metformin">Hold metformin 1000 mg twice daily</ref> while acidotic and NPO; resume when able.
5. Hypertension: <ref id="med_losartan">continue losartan 50 mg daily</ref>. Amlodipine, carvedilol and spironolactone also continue.
6. Glipizide, dulaglutide and rosuvastatin continue as listed.
