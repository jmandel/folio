## ED provider note

**History.** The patient came in with chest pain that began earlier today. The pain is not clearly related to exertion, and there has been no fainting. Allergy list includes empagliflozin (diabetic ketoacidosis). Home medications are metformin, dulaglutide, insulin glargine, lisinopril and amlodipine; none has been reconciled in the ED.

**Exam.** Vitals at 18:26: BP 148/89, HR 85, RR 19, T 99.2 F, SpO2 94%. The patient is comfortable at rest. Heart rhythm is regular, lungs are clear, and the chest wall is nontender.

**Results.** Basic metabolic panel at 18:40: sodium 137, potassium 4.1, chloride 102, bicarbonate 26, anion gap 9, urea nitrogen 13, creatinine 2.07, eGFR 34, glucose 124. <ref id="trop_1">Troponin I <0.01 on the 18:40 draw</ref> and <ref id="trop_2">troponin I <0.01 on the 21:40 draw</ref>. The ECG shows no ischemic changes.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_acs">Acute coronary syndrome ruled out</ref>: two troponins <0.01 three hours apart and an ECG without ischemic changes. The patient is stable for discharge from a cardiac standpoint.
2. Creatinine of 2.07 is the level seen on recent testing; no acute change is suspected.
3. Return precautions were reviewed: pain that returns or worsens, shortness of breath, or fainting. Follow up with the primary care physician within the week.
