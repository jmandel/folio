## Admission history and physical

**Reason for admission:** euglycemic diabetic ketoacidosis.

**History of present illness.** The patient came to the ED last night with weakness and shortness of breath after several days of vomiting and loose stools. <ref id="precipitant">The ketoacidosis was precipitated by a viral gastroenteritis with vomiting and almost no oral intake for several days.</ref> Abdominal pain was noted in the ED. Home medications include empagliflozin, metformin, dulaglutide, insulin glargine, lisinopril and amlodipine.

**Allergies:** NKDA.

**Exam.** Vitals at 05:57: BP 127/76, HR 89, RR 19, T 99 F, SpO2 95%. Mucous membranes are dry. Respirations are less labored than in the ED. Abdomen is soft.

**Results.** Admission basic metabolic panel (23:44): sodium 131, potassium 5.5, chloride 93, bicarbonate 11, anion gap 27, urea nitrogen 28, creatinine 2.39, eGFR 28, glucose 169. Lipase 45.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_dka">Euglycemic DKA, SGLT2 inhibitor-associated</ref>. Continue the insulin infusion per DKA protocol (started 00:30) with D5 lactated Ringer's at 150 mL/h, since glucose is near normal. Chemistries every 4 hours until the gap closes. <ref id="med_empagliflozin">Hold empagliflozin.</ref>
2. <ref id="dx_pancreatitis">Pancreatitis ruled out</ref>: lipase 45 and the abdominal pain has settled.
3. <ref id="med_metformin">Hold metformin</ref> while acidotic and NPO.
4. Hypertension and kidney injury: creatinine 2.39 with potassium 5.5. <ref id="med_lisinopril">Hold lisinopril</ref> for volume depletion and potassium; resume when eating. Amlodipine continues.
5. Dulaglutide and insulin glargine are listed to continue.
