## ED provider note

**History.** The patient came to the emergency department with a cough, feeling run down, and not eating well for several days. She has no known drug allergies. At home she takes metformin, empagliflozin, lisinopril and atorvastatin; metformin and empagliflozin are being held while she is here, and lisinopril and atorvastatin continue.

**Exam.** Vitals at 14:54: BP 134/80, HR 84, RR 20, T 98.4 F, SpO2 98%. She is comfortable and speaking in full sentences. Lung exam shows crackles consistent with a lung infection. Heart is regular. Abdomen is soft.

**Results.** <ref id="cbc">CBC drawn at 15:23: white blood cells 14.0, hemoglobin 12.3, platelets 237.</ref> The basic metabolic panel from the same draw showed sodium 131, potassium 4.7, chloride 97, bicarbonate 19, anion gap 15, urea nitrogen 48, creatinine 1.09, eGFR 56 and glucose 153.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_pneumonia">Community-acquired pneumonia</ref>, active. Ceftriaxone 1 g IV every 24 hours was started at 16:18. Her white count is raised, and her oxygen saturation is good on the readings so far.
2. Fluids: normal saline 75 mL/h was started at 15:38 and is still running, given the urea nitrogen of 48 and low sodium.
3. She will be admitted to the hospitalist service for further treatment. The patient understands and agrees with the plan.
