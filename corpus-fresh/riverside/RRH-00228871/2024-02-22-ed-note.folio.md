## ED provider note

**Chief complaint.** Weakness, nausea and fast breathing.

**History.** The patient presented feeling unwell with nausea and shortness of breath that has built over the day. Home medications are <ref id="med_empagliflozin">empagliflozin 10 mg daily</ref>, metformin 1000 mg twice daily with meals, glipizide 5 mg daily and levothyroxine 75 mcg daily. No known drug allergies.

**Exam.** At 13:54: BP 106/64, HR 110, RR 24, T 98.7 F, SpO2 98%. Ill-appearing but alert. Tachycardic with deep, rapid respirations. Mucous membranes dry. Abdomen soft.

**Results.** The <ref id="bmp">basic metabolic panel drawn at 14:07 showed sodium 128, potassium 5.0, chloride 95, bicarbonate 8, anion gap 25, urea nitrogen 33, creatinine 1.15, eGFR 58 and glucose 205</ref>. Venous blood gas drawn at 14:07: pH 7.15, pCO2 27. Beta-hydroxybutyrate 4.9.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_dka">Diabetic ketoacidosis, euglycemic</ref>. The glucose is only modestly raised, yet the pH is 7.15, bicarbonate 8, anion gap 25 and beta-hydroxybutyrate 4.9, which together make the diagnosis clear. Insulin infusion per DKA protocol at 0.1 units/kg/h ordered, with fluids. Repeat chemistry every few hours.
2. Sodium 128 and potassium 5.0 to be followed on serial panels, since insulin will move potassium.
3. Admit to the hospitalist service for continued management. Hospitalist informed.
