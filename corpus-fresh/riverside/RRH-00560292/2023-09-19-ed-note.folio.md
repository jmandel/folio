## ED provider note

**History.** The patient presented with several days of cough and feeling unwell, with fatigue and reduced appetite. There are no known drug allergies (NKDA). Home medications on the list are metformin 500 mg twice daily with meals, empagliflozin 10 mg daily, insulin glargine 10 units nightly, lisinopril 40 mg daily, chlorthalidone 12.5 mg (half of a 25 mg tablet) daily and amlodipine 5 mg daily. These have not yet been reconciled with the inpatient team.

**Exam.** Vitals at 17:40: BP 136/82, HR 83, RR 17, T 98.1 F, SpO2 95%. The patient is comfortable at rest, speaking in full sentences, without distress. Lung exam shows crackles at the base with otherwise unlabored breathing. Heart rhythm is regular. Abdomen is soft and nontender.

**Results.** <ref id="cbc">CBC drawn 17:55: white blood cells 18.0 (H), hemoglobin 13.9, platelets 226</ref>. On the basic metabolic panel from 17:55: sodium 136, potassium 4.8, chloride 98, bicarbonate 20 (L), anion gap 18 (H), urea nitrogen 55 (H), creatinine 2.48 (H), eGFR 23 (L), glucose 164 (H).

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_pneumonia">Community-acquired pneumonia</ref>, with leukocytosis and a clinical picture that fits. Ceftriaxone 1 g IV every 24 hours was started at 19:04 and is running.
2. Kidney function is abnormal, with creatinine 2.48 and urea nitrogen 55, and a mildly low bicarbonate with an elevated anion gap. Normal saline at 75 mL/h was started at 18:24 and is still running. Chemistries will be repeated.
3. The patient will be admitted to the hospitalist service for further treatment and monitoring. The patient and family understand the plan.
