## ED provider note

**History.** The patient presents after elective knee replacement surgery with nausea, belly discomfort and rapid breathing that began today. They fasted for a long period before the operation and have eaten little since. No fever and no vomiting of blood.

**Allergies:** lisinopril (angioedema).

**Home medications.** Metformin 1000 mg twice daily, glipizide 5 mg daily, losartan 100 mg daily, amlodipine 10 mg daily and atorvastatin 20 mg nightly are on the list and have not yet been reconciled. <ref id="med_dapagliflozin">Dapagliflozin 10 mg daily</ref> is also on the home list, not yet reconciled.

**Exam.** Vitals at 15:18: BP 106/64, HR 97, RR 21, T 98.3 F, SpO2 99%. The patient looks unwell, with deep, fast respirations and dry mucous membranes. Abdomen is soft with mild diffuse tenderness. Lungs are clear.

**Results.** <ref id="bmp">Basic metabolic panel drawn 15:28: sodium 131, potassium 4.8, chloride 92, bicarbonate 13, anion gap 26, urea nitrogen 37, creatinine 1.62, eGFR 45, glucose 199</ref>. Venous gas shows pH 7.09 and pCO2 23. Beta-hydroxybutyrate is 5.1. The wide anion gap acidosis with a near-normal glucose and high ketones is the pattern of ketoacidosis without marked hyperglycemia.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_dka">Diabetic ketoacidosis, euglycemic</ref>. The insulin infusion per DKA protocol at 0.1 units/kg/h was started at 16:22, with fluids. Potassium is 4.8, so we will follow it closely as insulin works.
2. Kidney function is mildly reduced, likely from volume depletion.
3. Disposition: admit to the hospitalist service for continued monitoring and repeat labs.
