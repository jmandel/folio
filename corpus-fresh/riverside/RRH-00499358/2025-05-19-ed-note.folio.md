## ED provider note

**History.** The patient came to the emergency department with several days of cough and some shortness of breath. They report feeling run down and have been eating less than usual. No known drug allergies.

**Home medications.** Metformin 1000 mg twice daily, dapagliflozin 10 mg daily, semaglutide 0.5 mg subcutaneously weekly, lisinopril 40 mg daily, chlorthalidone 12.5 mg daily, amlodipine 5 mg daily and atorvastatin 40 mg nightly. These have not yet been reconciled.

**Exam.** Vitals at 19:54: BP 145/87, HR 94, RR 20, T 98 F, SpO2 97%. Alert and conversant. Breath sounds are diminished with crackles at the base on one side. Heart rhythm regular. Abdomen soft.

**Results.** Labs were drawn at 20:22. <ref id="cbc">White blood cells 15.2 (H), hemoglobin 12.4 (L), platelets 195</ref>. Basic metabolic panel: sodium 136, potassium 4.3, chloride 101, bicarbonate 22, anion gap 13, urea nitrogen 56 (H), creatinine 2.04 (H), eGFR 34 (L), glucose 150 (H).

**Assessment and plan**

1. Clinical picture and labs are consistent with <ref id="dx_pneumonia">community-acquired pneumonia</ref>. Ceftriaxone 1 g IV every 24 h was started at 21:18.
2. Urea nitrogen and creatinine are elevated; normal saline at 75 mL/h was started at 20:38 and is running.
3. Admit to the hospitalist service for further care.
