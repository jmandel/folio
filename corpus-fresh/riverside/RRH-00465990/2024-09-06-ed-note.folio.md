## ED provider note

**Chief complaint.** Cough and shortness of breath.

**History.** The patient came to the ED with several days of cough, feeling feverish and tired, and breathlessness with walking. Appetite and fluid intake have been poor. No known drug allergies. Home medications are metformin 1000 mg twice daily, empagliflozin 10 mg daily, lisinopril 40 mg daily and amlodipine 5 mg daily; these have not yet been reconciled.

**Exam.** Vitals at 13:11: BP 143/86, HR 97, RR 21, T 99.2 F, SpO2 95%. Appears mildly ill but is speaking in full sentences. Breathing is slightly fast. Heart rhythm regular. Abdomen soft and non-tender.

**Results.** The 13:30 blood count showed <ref id="cbc">white blood cells 13.1, hemoglobin 14.0 and platelets 343</ref>. The basic metabolic panel from the same draw showed sodium 132, potassium 4.7, chloride 99, bicarbonate 21, anion gap 12, urea nitrogen 30, creatinine 2.70, eGFR 28 and glucose 125.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_pneumonia">Community-acquired pneumonia</ref>, with a raised white count and the symptoms above. Ceftriaxone 1 g IV every 24 hours was started at 14:35.
2. Reduced kidney function with low sodium and bicarbonate, likely related to poor intake. Normal saline at 75 mL/h was started at 13:55.
3. Admit to the hospitalist service for further treatment. Home medications to be reconciled by the admitting team.
