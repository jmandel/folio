## ED provider note

**Chief complaint.** Chest pain.

**History.** The patient presented with chest pain that began earlier today. No known drug allergies. Home medications are metformin 1000 mg twice daily, empagliflozin 10 mg daily, lisinopril 40 mg daily and amlodipine 5 mg daily; these have not been reconciled in the ED.

**Exam.** Vitals at 20:18: BP 165/99, HR 79, RR 24, T 98.1 F, SpO2 97%. Comfortable at rest, speaking in full sentences. Heart rhythm regular. Lungs clear. Chest wall and abdomen non-tender.

**Results.** The 20:36 basic metabolic panel showed sodium 140, potassium 4.1, chloride 105, bicarbonate 23, anion gap 12, urea nitrogen 16, creatinine 1.66, eGFR 50 and glucose 113. <ref id="trop_1">Troponin I <0.01</ref> on the 20:36 draw and <ref id="trop_2">troponin I <0.01</ref> on the repeat at 23:36.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_acs">Chest pain, acute coronary syndrome ruled out</ref>. Acute coronary syndrome ruled out: two troponins <0.01 three hours apart and an ECG without ischemic changes.
2. Blood pressure was elevated on arrival at 165/99; recheck before leaving the department and arrange outpatient follow-up.
3. Creatinine 1.66 with eGFR 50; follow up with primary care.
