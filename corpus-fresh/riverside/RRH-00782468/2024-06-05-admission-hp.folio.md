## Admission history and physical

**Reason for admission:** euglycemic diabetic ketoacidosis.

**History of present illness.** The patient is seen on the medical floor after evaluation in the ED this afternoon. <ref id="precipitant">The ketoacidosis was precipitated by a viral gastroenteritis with vomiting and almost no oral intake for several days.</ref> The patient came in feeling weak, thirsty and short of breath, and was found to be markedly acidotic with a glucose of only 219. There are no known drug allergies.

**Exam.** Vitals at 20:43: BP 104/62, HR 107, RR 21, T 98.2 F, SpO2 95%. The patient looks dry, with dry mucous membranes and deep, rapid respirations. Abdomen is soft and nontender. Alert and answers questions appropriately.

**Results.** On the 15:10 panel sodium 134, potassium 5.5, chloride 92, bicarbonate 13, anion gap 29, urea nitrogen 33, creatinine 1.27 (eGFR 66), glucose 219. Lipase 40.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_dka">Euglycemic DKA, SGLT2 inhibitor-associated</ref>. Continue the insulin infusion per DKA protocol at 0.1 units/kg/h, started 16:05, together with D5 lactated Ringer's at 150 mL/h, since glucose is not high. Recheck the metabolic panel serially until the anion gap closes. <ref id="med_empagliflozin">Hold empagliflozin 10 mg daily.</ref>
2. <ref id="dx_pancreatitis">Pancreatitis ruled out</ref>: lipase 40, abdomen benign.
3. Type 2 diabetes: <ref id="med_metformin">hold metformin</ref> while acidotic and NPO.
4. Hypertension and elevated potassium: <ref id="med_losartan">hold losartan</ref> for volume depletion and potassium 5.5; resume when eating.
5. Continue atorvastatin 20 mg nightly.
