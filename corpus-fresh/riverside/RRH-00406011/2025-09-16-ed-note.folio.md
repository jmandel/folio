## ED provider note

**History.** The patient came to the ED in the evening with several days of cough and breathlessness on exertion, with some tiredness. There was no chest pain. No allergies are on the list (NKDA). Home medications on the list are metformin 1000 mg twice daily with meals, dapagliflozin 10 mg daily, losartan 100 mg daily, chlorthalidone 12.5 mg (half of a 25 mg tablet) daily, amlodipine 5 mg daily and atorvastatin 20 mg nightly; none has been reconciled yet.

**Exam.** Vitals at 23:40: BP 124/74, HR 84, RR 19, T 98.7 F, SpO2 94%. The patient was comfortable at rest and spoke in full sentences. Breath sounds were diminished with crackles at the bases. Heart rhythm was regular. Abdomen soft and nontender.

**Results.** The <ref id="cbc">CBC drawn at 23:53 showed white blood cells 12.5 (H), hemoglobin 12.9 (L) and platelets 278</ref>. Basic metabolic panel from 23:53: sodium 136, potassium 4.9, chloride 103, bicarbonate 21 (L), anion gap 12, urea nitrogen 35 (H), creatinine 2.27 (H), eGFR 31 (L), glucose 198 (H).

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_pneumonia">Community-acquired pneumonia</ref>, based on the cough, the crackles on exam and the raised white count. Normal saline was started at 75 mL/h at 00:24 and ceftriaxone 1 g IV every 24 hours was started at 01:04; both are still running. The patient will be admitted to the hospitalist service.
2. Creatinine 2.27 with urea nitrogen 35 and bicarbonate 21 on the 23:53 panel; fluids are running as above and the hospitalist team will follow the numbers.
3. Home medications to be reconciled by the admitting team.
