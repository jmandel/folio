## ED provider note

**History.** The patient presented on 6/16 with several days of feeling unwell, nausea, and fast breathing. They take <ref id="med_empagliflozin">empagliflozin 10 mg daily</ref> at home; home medications have not yet been reconciled. Other home medications on the list are metformin 1000 mg twice daily, lisinopril 40 mg daily, hydrochlorothiazide 25 mg daily, amlodipine 5 mg daily and atorvastatin 40 mg nightly. Allergies: NKDA.

**Exam.** At 13:18: BP 100/60, HR 83, RR 16, T 99 F, SpO2 96%. Appears uncomfortable but alert. Mucous membranes are dry. Abdomen is soft with mild diffuse tenderness and no guarding.

**Results.** Venous blood gas on the 13:45 draw showed pH 7.18 and pCO2 30. Beta-hydroxybutyrate 6.2. <ref id="bmp">Basic metabolic panel: sodium 130, potassium 5.6, chloride 94, bicarbonate 8, anion gap 28, urea nitrogen 30, creatinine 1.73, eGFR 46, glucose 242.</ref>

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_dka">Diabetic ketoacidosis, euglycemic</ref>: glucose is only modestly elevated but there is a profound anion gap acidosis with ketonemia, in a patient on an SGLT2 inhibitor. An insulin infusion per DKA protocol is being started, with IV fluids. Admit to the hospitalist service.
2. <ref id="dx_pancreatitis">R/O pancreatitis</ref> given the abdominal tenderness; lipase is pending.
3. Hyperkalemia with potassium 5.6 in the setting of acidosis and insulin deficiency; recheck with the next chemistry as insulin takes effect.
