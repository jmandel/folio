## ED provider note

**History.** The patient came to the ED with cough, fever sensations and shortness of breath over the past few days. The symptoms were enough to bring them in today. Allergies: NKDA. Home medications were listed but not yet reconciled: metformin 1000 mg twice daily with meals, empagliflozin 10 mg daily, insulin glargine 10 units nightly, and atorvastatin 20 mg nightly.

**Exam.** Vitals at 17:40: BP 133/80, HR 88, RR 22, T 98.8 F, SpO2 99%. Alert and conversant, mildly tachypneic with a productive-sounding cough. No acute distress.

**Results.** <ref id="cbc">CBC drawn at 17:50: white blood cells 15.2, hemoglobin 12.9, platelets 263.</ref> Basic metabolic panel from the same draw: sodium 131, potassium 4.5, chloride 103, bicarbonate 18, anion gap 10, urea nitrogen 60, creatinine 1.98 with eGFR 30, glucose 180.

**ED course.** Normal saline was started at 75 mL/h at 18:24. Ceftriaxone 1 g IV every 24 hours was started at 19:04 and is still running.

**Assessment and plan.** The picture is consistent with <ref id="dx_pneumonia">community-acquired pneumonia</ref>, supported by the raised white count and the symptoms described. The patient will be admitted to the hospitalist service for continued treatment and monitoring. Home medication reconciliation is left to the admitting team.
