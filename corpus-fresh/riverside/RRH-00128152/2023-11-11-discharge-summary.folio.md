## Discharge summary

**Diagnosis.** <ref id="dx_dka">Euglycemic DKA, SGLT2 inhibitor-associated, resolved</ref>.

**Hospital course.** <ref id="precipitant">The ketoacidosis was precipitated by a viral gastroenteritis with vomiting and almost no oral intake for several days.</ref> He presented acidotic with a glucose that was near normal. He was treated with an insulin infusion and dextrose-containing fluids, then transitioned to subcutaneous insulin, and his acidosis resolved. He is now eating and drinking normally and feels well.

**Condition at discharge.** Vitals at 10:30: BP 128/77, HR 79, RR 20, T 99.2 F, SpO2 96%. The discharge basic metabolic panel at 06:21 on 11/11 showed sodium 132, potassium 4.5, chloride 100, bicarbonate 19, anion gap 13, urea nitrogen 24, creatinine 1.21 (eGFR 70) and glucose 149.

**Allergies:** NKDA.

**Discharge medications**

- <ref id="med_dapagliflozin">Dapagliflozin 10 mg daily: do not resume until reviewed by his PCP</ref>
- <ref id="med_metformin">Metformin 1000 mg twice daily with meals: continue</ref>
- <ref id="med_lisinopril">Lisinopril 10 mg daily: continue</ref>
- <ref id="med_hydrochlorothiazide">Hydrochlorothiazide 25 mg daily: continue</ref>
- Atorvastatin 20 mg nightly: continue

**Follow-up.** Primary care at Northgate Family Medicine within 1 week, to decide whether to restart dapagliflozin and to recheck a basic metabolic panel.
