## ED provider note

**Chief complaint.** Nausea, weakness and fast breathing.

**History.** The patient is a man with type 2 diabetes and hypertension who came to the ED after several days of feeling unwell, with poor oral intake and ongoing weakness. He takes <ref id="med_dapagliflozin">dapagliflozin 10 mg daily</ref> along with metformin 1000 mg twice daily, lisinopril 10 mg daily, hydrochlorothiazide 25 mg daily and atorvastatin 20 mg nightly. Allergies: NKDA.

**Exam.** Vitals at 17:18: BP 112/67, HR 95, RR 22, T 98 F, SpO2 98%. He looks tired and mildly dehydrated, with deep, rapid respirations. The abdomen is soft and non-peritoneal.

**Results.** Venous blood gas showed pH 7.09 and pCO2 26. Beta-hydroxybutyrate was 6.6. The <ref id="bmp">basic metabolic panel drawn at 17:39 showed sodium 134, potassium 5.4, chloride 94, bicarbonate 8, anion gap 32, urea nitrogen 35, creatinine 1.43, eGFR 58 and glucose 179</ref>.

**Assessment and plan.** <ref id="dx_dka">Euglycemic diabetic ketoacidosis</ref>: a wide anion gap metabolic acidosis with markedly elevated ketones despite a glucose of only 179. An insulin infusion per DKA protocol at 0.1 units/kg/h was ordered and started at 18:22. He will be admitted to the hospitalist service for continued treatment and monitoring.
