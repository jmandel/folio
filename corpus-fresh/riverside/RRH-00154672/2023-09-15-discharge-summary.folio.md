## Discharge summary

**Diagnoses.** <ref id="dx_dka">Euglycemic DKA, SGLT2 inhibitor-associated, resolved.</ref> <ref id="dx_pancreatitis">Acute pancreatitis was ruled out</ref> during the stay.

**Hospital course.** The patient presented with nausea, malaise and fast breathing and was found to have ketoacidosis with a near-normal glucose. <ref id="precipitant">The ketoacidosis was precipitated by a weekend of heavy alcohol use with little food.</ref> They were treated with an insulin infusion and fluids, then moved to subcutaneous insulin once the anion gap closed. Symptoms resolved and they are eating.

**Condition at discharge.** Vitals at 09:15: BP 128/77, HR 103, RR 16, T 99.1 F, SpO2 98%. The basic metabolic panel at 06:05 on 9/15 showed sodium 137, potassium 3.9, chloride 104, bicarbonate 19, anion gap 14, urea nitrogen 25, creatinine 0.88, eGFR 74 and glucose 186.

**Discharge medications**

- <ref id="med_empagliflozin">Empagliflozin 10 mg daily: do not resume until reviewed by primary care.</ref>
- <ref id="med_metformin">Metformin 1000 mg twice daily with meals: continue.</ref>
- <ref id="med_losartan">Losartan 100 mg daily: continue.</ref>
- Glipizide 5 mg daily: continue.
- Dulaglutide 0.5 mg weekly: continue.
- Insulin glargine 14 units nightly: continue.
- Rosuvastatin 20 mg nightly: continue.

**Allergies.** Lisinopril (cough).

**Follow-up.** Primary care at Northgate Family Medicine within 1 week to decide whether to restart empagliflozin and to recheck a basic metabolic panel.
