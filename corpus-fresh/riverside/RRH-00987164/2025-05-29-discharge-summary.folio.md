## Discharge summary

**Diagnosis:** <ref id="dx_dka">euglycemic DKA, SGLT2 inhibitor-associated, resolved</ref>.

**Hospital course.** The patient came to the hospital with nausea, fatigue and rapid, deep breathing and was found to have ketoacidosis with a near-normal glucose. <ref id="precipitant">The ketoacidosis was precipitated by elective knee replacement surgery with prolonged pre-operative fasting while the SGLT2 inhibitor was continued.</ref> Treatment was an insulin infusion with dextrose-containing fluids per DKA protocol, followed by a transition to subcutaneous insulin. The acidosis and anion gap resolved, and the patient was eating and feeling well by the time of discharge.

**Discharge data.** Vitals at 08:30: BP 122/73, HR 86, RR 23, T 98.8 F, SpO2 96%. Basic metabolic panel on 5/29 at 06:00: sodium 137, potassium 4.1, chloride 100, bicarbonate 23, anion gap 14, urea nitrogen 17, creatinine 1.09, eGFR 75, glucose 147.

**Allergies:** no known drug allergies.

**Discharge medications**

- <ref id="med_dapagliflozin">Dapagliflozin 10 mg daily: held. Do not resume until reviewed by the PCP.</ref>
- <ref id="med_metformin">Metformin 1000 mg twice daily with meals: continue.</ref>
- <ref id="med_losartan">Losartan 100 mg daily: continue.</ref>
- <ref id="med_furosemide">Furosemide 40 mg daily: continue.</ref>
- Carvedilol 12.5 mg twice daily: continue.
- Spironolactone 25 mg daily: continue.
- Rosuvastatin 20 mg nightly: continue.

**Follow-up.** Primary care at Northgate Family Medicine within 1 week, to decide whether to restart dapagliflozin and to recheck a basic metabolic panel. The patient was told to return for vomiting, abdominal pain, fast breathing or marked weakness.
