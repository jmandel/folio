## ED provider note

**History.** The patient came to the emergency department with several days of cough and feeling unwell, with some shortness of breath on exertion. There was no chest pain. Allergy list: NKDA. Home medications have not yet been reconciled; the list on file includes metformin 1000 mg twice daily, dapagliflozin 10 mg daily, dulaglutide 0.5 mg weekly, insulin glargine 10 units nightly, lisinopril 40 mg daily, chlorthalidone 12.5 mg daily, amlodipine 5 mg daily and atorvastatin 40 mg nightly.

**Exam.** Vitals at 20:26: BP 124/74, HR 99, RR 19, T 98.7 F, SpO2 97%. The patient was comfortable at rest and speaking in full sentences. Breath sounds were diminished with crackles at the base of one lung. Heart rhythm regular. Abdomen soft and nontender.

**Results.** The <ref id="cbc">complete blood count drawn 20:48 showed white blood cells 13.5 (H), hemoglobin 13.4 and platelets 351</ref>. The basic metabolic panel from the same draw showed sodium 131 (L), potassium 4.8, chloride 102, bicarbonate 21 (L), anion gap 8, urea nitrogen 58 (H), creatinine 1.49 (H), eGFR 40 (L) and glucose 171 (H). The elevated urea nitrogen and creatinine with a low sodium suggest the patient is dry after several days of poor intake.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_pneumonia">Community-acquired pneumonia</ref>, based on the cough, the focal crackles and the raised white count. Ceftriaxone 1 g IV every 24 hours was started at 21:50 and is running.
2. Volume depletion with abnormal kidney function and low sodium. Normal saline at 75 mL/h was started at 21:10 and is still running. We will repeat the basic metabolic panel in the morning.
3. Home medications are not yet reconciled; this will be completed by the admitting team.

The patient is being admitted to the hospitalist service for IV antibiotics and fluids. The patient was updated on the plan and agrees.
