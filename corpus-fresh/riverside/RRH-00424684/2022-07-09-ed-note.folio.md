## ED provider note

**History.** The patient was seen in the emergency department on the evening of 7/9 with an acute illness and was evaluated for a respiratory infection. Symptoms were reviewed with the patient, who was able to give a clear account. Allergy list: NKDA. Home medications on file are metformin 500 mg twice daily with meals, glipizide 5 mg daily, losartan 100 mg daily and rosuvastatin 20 mg nightly; none have been reconciled yet in this visit.

**Exam.** Vitals at 19:40: BP 147/88, HR 96, RR 17, T 98.8 F, SpO2 98%. The patient was comfortable at rest, speaking in full sentences, without distress.

**Results.** The <ref id="cbc">complete blood count drawn at 19:59 showed white blood cells 14.7 (H), hemoglobin 13.4 (L), platelets 204</ref>. The basic metabolic panel from the same draw: sodium 138, potassium 4.5, chloride 96 (L), bicarbonate 20 (L), anion gap 22 (H), urea nitrogen 60 (H), creatinine 1.53 (H), eGFR 53 (L), glucose 124 (H).

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_pneumonia">Community-acquired pneumonia</ref>. Normal saline was started at 75 mL/h at 20:24 and is still running. Ceftriaxone 1 g IV every 24 hours was started at 21:04 and is also running. The patient will be handed off to the hospitalist team for admission.
2. Abnormal chemistry: bicarbonate, anion gap, urea nitrogen and creatinine are abnormal as listed above; fluids are running and the inpatient team will follow the panel.
