## Admission history and physical

**Reason for admission:** euglycemic diabetic ketoacidosis.

**History of present illness.** The patient came to the ED on the evening of 11/14 with malaise, nausea and fast, deep breathing, and was found to be acidotic with a glucose of only 178. No clear precipitant was identified: no infection, no missed meals reported, no alcohol, no recent surgery. There is no known drug allergy.

**Exam.** Vitals at 01:14: BP 108/65, HR 80, RR 21, T 98.6 F, SpO2 96%. Mucous membranes are dry. Respirations are slightly deep but unlabored. Abdomen soft and nontender.

**Results.** The 19:52 panel showed sodium 135, potassium 4.8, chloride 99, bicarbonate 12, anion gap 24, urea nitrogen 35, creatinine 1.29, eGFR 54 and glucose 178.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_dka">Euglycemic DKA, SGLT2 inhibitor-associated</ref>. The insulin infusion per DKA protocol (0.1 units/kg/h) and D5 lactated Ringer's at 150 mL/h, both started 20:30, continue, with D5 used because glucose is near normal. Chemistry checks are to continue until the gap closes. <ref id="med_dapagliflozin">Dapagliflozin is held.</ref>
2. Diabetes: <ref id="med_metformin">metformin is held while she is acidotic and NPO</ref>, to be resumed when able.
3. Volume status: <ref id="med_furosemide">furosemide is held for volume depletion</ref>, to be resumed when able.
4. Cardiac and blood pressure: <ref id="med_lisinopril">lisinopril is continued</ref>, as are carvedilol and spironolactone. BP is soft-normal and potassium is 4.8, so we will watch both closely.
5. Atorvastatin is continued.
