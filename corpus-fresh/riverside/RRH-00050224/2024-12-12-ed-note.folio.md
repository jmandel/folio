## Emergency department provider note

**History.** The patient came to the ED on the evening of 12/12 with chest pain. The pain was evaluated on arrival and the patient was placed on a monitor. There was no fainting and no report of ongoing distress at the time of my evaluation. The home medication list was reviewed, but none of the home medications have been reconciled in the ED: metformin 500 mg twice daily with meals, empagliflozin 10 mg daily, insulin glargine 10 units nightly, lisinopril 40 mg daily, and chlorthalidone 12.5 mg daily. No known drug allergies.

**Exam.** Vitals at 23:40: BP 144/86, HR 108, RR 22, T 98.7 F, SpO2 94%. The patient was mildly tachycardic and breathing a little fast but spoke in full sentences. Heart sounds regular without a murmur. Lungs without wheeze. Abdomen soft. No leg swelling.

**Results.** The ECG showed no ischemic changes. The first troponin, drawn at 23:58, was <ref id="trop_1">Troponin I <0.01 ng/mL</ref>. The repeat troponin, drawn at 02:58, was <ref id="trop_2">Troponin I <0.01 ng/mL</ref>. The basic metabolic panel drawn at 23:58 showed sodium 138, potassium 4.3, chloride 99, bicarbonate 25, anion gap 14, urea nitrogen 12, creatinine 1.73 (H), eGFR 36 (L), and glucose 163 (H).

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_acs">Acute coronary syndrome ruled out</ref>: two troponins <0.01 three hours apart and an ECG without ischemic changes. The chest pain is therefore not explained by a cardiac ischemic event on this evaluation.
2. Creatinine is 1.73 with an eGFR of 36 on the 23:58 panel; this is noted for the next clinician to review alongside the home medication list, which remains unreconciled.
3. Heart rate and respiratory rate were mildly elevated on arrival; the patient was reassessed after the serial troponins and was comfortable at the time of this note.

The patient and family were told the results and what they mean. Follow-up with the primary care physician in the next few days is recommended, and the patient should return for any new or worsening chest pain, shortness of breath, or fainting.
