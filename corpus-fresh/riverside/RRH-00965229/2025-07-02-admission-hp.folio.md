## Admission history and physical

**Reason for admission:** euglycemic diabetic ketoacidosis.

**History of present illness.** The patient came in the evening of 7/1 with nausea, fatigue and weakness and was found to be acidotic with a glucose of only 215. No clear precipitant was identified: no infection, no missed meals reported, no alcohol, no recent surgery. Allergies: NKDA.

**Medications at home.** <ref id="med_dapagliflozin">Dapagliflozin 10 mg daily</ref> is held. Metformin, losartan and furosemide are addressed below. Glipizide 5 mg daily, carvedilol 12.5 mg twice daily, spironolactone 25 mg daily and atorvastatin 40 mg nightly are continued.

**Exam.** Vitals at 03:21: BP 107/64, HR 98, RR 20, T 99 F, SpO2 96%. Mildly dry mucous membranes, breathing a little deeply but comfortable. Abdomen soft.

**Results.** Sodium 131, potassium 5.2, bicarbonate 10, anion gap 22, urea nitrogen 31, creatinine 1.63, glucose 215 on the 21:39 panel. Lipase 37.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_dka">Euglycemic DKA, SGLT2 inhibitor-associated</ref>. Continue the insulin infusion per DKA protocol (started 22:15) with D5 lactated Ringer's at 150 mL/h, since glucose is near normal. Hold dapagliflozin.
2. <ref id="dx_pancreatitis">Acute pancreatitis ruled out</ref>: lipase 37, no significant abdominal findings.
3. Diabetes: <ref id="med_metformin">hold metformin</ref> while acidotic and NPO.
4. Volume depletion with high potassium: <ref id="med_losartan">hold losartan</ref> and resume when eating. <ref id="med_furosemide">Hold furosemide</ref> for volume depletion; resume when able.
5. Continue the other home medications as listed above.
