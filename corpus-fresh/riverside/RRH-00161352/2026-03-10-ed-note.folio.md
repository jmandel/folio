## ED provider note

**Chief complaint.** Several days of fever, aches, nausea and poor oral intake, now feeling weak and short of breath.

**History.** The patient has diabetes and took their usual medications until intake fell off, including <ref id="med_dapagliflozin">dapagliflozin 10 mg daily</ref>. Other home medications are metformin 500 mg twice daily, insulin glargine 14 units nightly and atorvastatin 40 mg nightly. No known drug allergies. Medication reconciliation is not yet complete.

**Exam.** Vitals at 16:54: BP 113/68, HR 89, RR 21, T 99.1 F, SpO2 97%. The patient looks tired and dry, with deep respirations. The abdomen is soft.

**Results.** Venous blood gas at 17:04: pH 7.08, pCO2 25. Beta-hydroxybutyrate 5.2. <ref id="bmp">Basic metabolic panel at 17:04: sodium 132, potassium 5.5, chloride 100, bicarbonate 13, anion gap 19, urea nitrogen 30, creatinine 2.90, eGFR 23, glucose 211.</ref>

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_dka">Diabetic ketoacidosis, euglycemic</ref>: marked acidosis with an elevated anion gap and ketones despite a glucose of only 211. An insulin infusion per DKA protocol at 0.1 units/kg/h was started at 17:58, with IV fluids.
2. <ref id="dx_pancreatitis">R/O pancreatitis</ref> given the nausea; lipase is pending at the time of this note.
3. Elevated potassium and creatinine on the panel; repeat labs after treatment starts.
4. Admit to the hospitalist service.
