## Admission history and physical

**Reason for admission:** euglycemic diabetic ketoacidosis.

**History of present illness.** The patient has type 2 diabetes and hypertension and was sent from the ED with acidosis and a near-normal glucose. <ref id="precipitant">The ketoacidosis was precipitated by a viral gastroenteritis with vomiting and almost no oral intake for several days.</ref> No known drug allergies.

**Exam.** Vitals at 02:52: BP 119/71, HR 87, RR 23, T 98.4 F, SpO2 94%. Mildly dry mucous membranes, breathing deep, abdomen soft and nontender at this point.

**Results.** Sodium 133, potassium 5.5, bicarbonate 8, anion gap 26, urea nitrogen 29, creatinine 1.17, glucose 216 on the 21:14 panel. Lipase 27 on the same draw.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_dka">Euglycemic DKA, SGLT2 inhibitor-associated</ref>. Insulin infusion per DKA protocol and D5 lactated Ringer's at 150 mL/h, both started 22:05 and running, with the dextrose included because glucose is near normal. <ref id="med_empagliflozin">Empagliflozin is held.</ref>
2. <ref id="dx_pancreatitis">Acute pancreatitis ruled out</ref>: lipase 27.
3. <ref id="med_metformin">Metformin is held</ref> while acidotic and NPO; resume when able.
4. <ref id="med_losartan">Losartan is held</ref> for volume depletion and the high potassium; resume when eating.
5. <ref id="med_hydrochlorothiazide">Hydrochlorothiazide is held</ref> for volume depletion; resume when able.
6. Other home medications (glipizide, semaglutide, amlodipine, atorvastatin) continue as listed.
