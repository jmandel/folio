## ED provider note

**Chief complaint.** Cough and weakness.

**History.** The patient presents with several days of cough, feeling run down and poor oral intake. They have type 2 diabetes and hypertension. Allergies: NKDA. The home medications on file are metformin 500 mg twice daily, dapagliflozin 10 mg daily, lisinopril 40 mg daily, hydrochlorothiazide 25 mg daily and atorvastatin 20 mg nightly. None of the home medications has been reconciled in the ED yet.

**Exam.** Vitals at 16:47: BP 145/87, HR 92, RR 18, T 98.7 F, SpO2 96%. Alert and conversant, not in distress. Dry mucous membranes. Lungs with crackles at one base. Abdomen soft. No focal neurologic findings.

**Results.** The <ref id="cbc">complete blood count drawn at 17:01 shows white blood cells 13.2, hemoglobin 13.9 and platelets 205</ref>. The basic metabolic panel from the same draw shows sodium 130, potassium 4.9, chloride 103, bicarbonate 17, anion gap 10, urea nitrogen 51, creatinine 1.63, eGFR 46 and glucose 194.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_pneumonia">Community-acquired pneumonia</ref>. Ceftriaxone 1 g IV every 24 hours was started at 18:11.
2. Dehydration with kidney function worse than expected and low sodium. Normal saline at 75 mL/h was started at 17:31 and continues.
3. Home medications are still to be reconciled by the admitting team.

The patient is being admitted to the hospitalist service, and the plan was reviewed with them at the bedside.
