## ED provider note

**Chief complaint.** Vomiting, abdominal discomfort and fast breathing.

**History.** The patient has type 2 diabetes and hypertension and describes several days of vomiting and poor intake, with growing weakness. Allergies: NKDA. Home medications on file are metformin 500 mg twice daily, lisinopril 40 mg daily, hydrochlorothiazide 25 mg daily, atorvastatin 20 mg nightly and <ref id="med_dapagliflozin">dapagliflozin 10 mg daily</ref>; none has been reconciled in the ED.

**Exam.** Vitals at 14:40: BP 102/61, HR 79, RR 16, T 99 F, SpO2 95%. Ill-appearing, dry mucous membranes, deep breathing. Abdomen soft with mild diffuse tenderness.

**Results.** Venous blood gas at 15:01 shows pH 7.12 and pCO2 22. Beta-hydroxybutyrate is 5.9. <ref id="bmp">Basic metabolic panel at 15:01: sodium 134, potassium 5.3, chloride 98, bicarbonate 14, anion gap 22, urea nitrogen 27, creatinine 1.53, eGFR 50, glucose 206.</ref> The glucose is only mildly elevated against a marked acidosis.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_dka">Diabetic ketoacidosis, euglycemic</ref>. Insulin infusion per DKA protocol at 0.1 units/kg/h was started at 15:44 and continues.
2. Dehydration with mildly raised potassium and creatinine; to be rechecked on serial panels.
3. Admit to the hospitalist service. Medication reconciliation will be completed on admission.
