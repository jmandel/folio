## Emergency department provider note

**History.** The patient presented this evening with a several-day illness of cough, fatigue and feeling short of breath with exertion. No known drug allergies. Home medications are listed below but have not yet been reconciled with the patient or pharmacy.

**Exam.** Vitals at 20:47: BP 145/87, HR 81, RR 24, T 98.7 F, SpO2 94%. The patient is alert and speaking in full sentences, with a mildly increased respiratory rate. Breath sounds are diminished with crackles at the base on the affected side. Heart rhythm is regular. Abdomen is soft and nontender.

**Results.** The <ref id="cbc">complete blood count at 21:13 showed white blood cells 14.5 (H), hemoglobin 14.0 and platelets 217</ref>. The basic metabolic panel drawn at 21:13 showed sodium 129 (L), potassium 4.4, chloride 104, bicarbonate 20 (L), anion gap 5 (L), urea nitrogen 42 (H), creatinine 1.39 (H), eGFR 40 (L) and glucose 145 (H).

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_pneumonia">Community-acquired pneumonia</ref>, based on the symptoms, the chest findings and the raised white count. Normal saline was started at 75 mL/h at 21:31 and is still running. Ceftriaxone 1 g IV every 24 hours was started at 22:11 and is running as ordered.
2. Low sodium and elevated urea nitrogen and creatinine on the first panel, consistent with volume depletion in the setting of acute illness. Fluids are running and the panel will be repeated.
3. Home medications on file: metformin 1000 mg twice daily with meals, losartan 50 mg daily, hydrochlorothiazide 25 mg daily and atorvastatin 40 mg nightly. All are awaiting reconciliation.

The patient is being admitted to the hospitalist service, and the plan was discussed with the patient, who agrees.
