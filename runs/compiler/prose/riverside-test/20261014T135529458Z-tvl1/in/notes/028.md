## ED provider note

**Chief complaint:** cough and shortness of breath.

**History.** The patient presented this afternoon after several days of cough and feeling run down, with little to eat or drink. She has no known drug allergies. Her home medications are metformin 1000 mg twice daily, dapagliflozin 10 mg daily, lisinopril 20 mg daily and rosuvastatin 20 mg nightly; none have been reconciled yet in the ED.

**Exam.** At 18:01: BP 144/86, HR 80, RR 24, T 99.1 F, SpO2 97%. She is mildly tachypneic but speaking in full sentences. Crackles are heard on lung exam. Abdomen soft and non-tender.

**Results.** The <ref id="cbc">CBC drawn at 18:27 showed white blood cells 17.7, hemoglobin 13.3 and platelets 274</ref>. The basic metabolic panel from the same draw showed sodium 134, potassium 4.3, chloride 97, bicarbonate 19, anion gap 18, urea nitrogen 59, creatinine 1.63 (eGFR 50) and glucose 114.

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_pneumonia">Community-acquired pneumonia</ref>, based on her symptoms, exam and the elevated white count. Ceftriaxone 1 g IV every 24 hours was started at 19:25 and is still running.
2. Elevated urea nitrogen and creatinine with a raised anion gap, likely from poor oral intake. Normal saline at 75 mL/h was started at 18:45 and is still running.
3. Admit to the hospitalist service for continued treatment. Home medications are to be reconciled on admission.
