## ED provider note

**Chief complaint.** Cough and feeling unwell.

**History.** The patient came to the emergency department with a cough and general malaise. Home medications are metformin 500 mg twice daily, lisinopril 20 mg daily, amlodipine 5 mg daily and atorvastatin 40 mg nightly. There are no known drug allergies. Home medications have not yet been reconciled in the ED.

**Exam.** Vitals at 14:26: BP 139/83, HR 91, RR 17, T 98.4 F, SpO2 97%. The patient appears mildly ill but is not in distress and is breathing comfortably.

**Results.** The <ref id="cbc">white blood cell count is 16.2, with hemoglobin 13.9 and platelets 204</ref>, drawn at 14:36. Basic metabolic panel from the same draw: sodium 137, potassium 4.3, chloride 106, bicarbonate 22, anion gap 9, urea nitrogen 58 (high), creatinine 1.64 (high), eGFR 44 (low), glucose 146 (high).

**ED course.** Normal saline was started at 75 mL/h at 15:10 because of the elevated urea nitrogen and creatinine. Ceftriaxone 1 g IV every 24 hours was started at 15:50 and is still running.

**Assessment and plan.** The picture fits <ref id="dx_pneumonia">community-acquired pneumonia</ref>, with the elevated white count supporting an infectious process. The patient will be admitted to the hospitalist service for continued intravenous antibiotics and fluids, and the hospitalist will reconcile the home medications.
