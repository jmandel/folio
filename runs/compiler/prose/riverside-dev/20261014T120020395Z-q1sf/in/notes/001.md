## ED provider note

**Chief complaint.** Cough and feeling unwell.

**History.** The patient came to the emergency department with several days of cough and malaise. The cough has been bothersome and the patient has not been eating or drinking well. No known drug allergies.

**Exam.** Vitals at 14:40: BP 141/85, HR 87, RR 16, T 98.2 F, SpO2 94% on room air. The patient is alert and appears tired but not in distress. Breath sounds are diminished at the base with some crackles. Heart rate is regular and the abdomen is soft.

**Results.** The <ref id="cbc">CBC drawn at 14:52 showed white blood cells 13.7 (H), hemoglobin 13.8 and platelets 313</ref>. The basic metabolic panel from the same draw showed sodium 130 (L), potassium 4.5, chloride 100, bicarbonate 18 (L), anion gap 12, urea nitrogen 50 (H), creatinine 1.69 (H) with an eGFR of 42 (L), and glucose 117 (H).

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_pneumonia">Community-acquired pneumonia</ref>, based on the cough, the chest findings and the raised white count. Ceftriaxone 1 g IV every 24 hours was started at 16:04 and is running.
2. Low sodium, low bicarbonate, and elevated urea nitrogen and creatinine: normal saline 0.9% at 75 mL/h was started at 15:24 and continues. Labs will be repeated in the morning.
3. Disposition: the patient will be admitted to the hospitalist service for continued treatment. The plan was explained and the patient agrees.
