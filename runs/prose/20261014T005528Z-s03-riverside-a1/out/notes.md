**Results.** The basic metabolic panel drawn at 14:20 (<ref id="bmp">sodium 130, potassium 6.4, chloride 104, bicarbonate 16, anion gap 10, urea nitrogen 48, creatinine 1.67, eGFR 43, glucose 210</ref>) shows a markedly high potassium and a raised creatinine.

**Course and assessment.**

The picture is <ref id="dx_aki">acute kidney injury</ref> with <ref id="dx_hyperk">hyperkalemia</ref>, most likely from volume loss. For the high potassium I gave calcium gluconate 1 g IV, started at 14:35 and finished at 14:45. Normal saline 1000 mL bolus was started at 14:45, then 125 mL/h, and is still running. The patient tolerated both without difficulty.

Home medications were not yet reconciled in the ED and will be addressed by the admitting team. I discussed the findings with the patient, who agrees with admission to the hospitalist service for IV fluids and repeat labs.
