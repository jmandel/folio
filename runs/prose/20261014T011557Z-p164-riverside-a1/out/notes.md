## Emergency department provider note

**Chief complaint.** Chest pain.

**History.** The patient came to the ED this evening with chest pain that began earlier today. They describe it as a pressure-like discomfort in the center of the chest, without radiation that they could identify. They have no known drug allergies. Home medications listed are metformin 1000 mg twice daily with meals, empagliflozin 10 mg daily, lisinopril 40 mg daily and atorvastatin 20 mg nightly; these have not yet been reconciled.

**Exam.** Vitals at 19:18: BP 143/86, HR 96, RR 24, T 98.3 F, SpO2 98%. The patient was comfortable at rest and spoke in full sentences. Heart regular, lungs clear, chest wall without a reproducible source of pain, abdomen soft.

**Results.** The ECG showed no ischemic changes. Troponin I was <0.01 ng/mL on the first draw (<ref id="trop_1">Troponin I <0.01 ng/mL at 19:38</ref>) and again <0.01 ng/mL on the repeat (<ref id="trop_2">Troponin I <0.01 ng/mL at 22:38</ref>). Basic metabolic panel at 19:38: sodium 136, potassium 4.5, chloride 99, bicarbonate 28, anion gap 9, urea nitrogen 17, creatinine 1.05, eGFR 84, glucose 139 (H).

**Assessment and plan**

1. <ref id="dx_acs">Acute coronary syndrome ruled out</ref>: two troponins <0.01 three hours apart and an ECG without ischemic changes. The chemistry panel is otherwise reassuring, with a normal anion gap and preserved kidney function.
2. The results and the reasoning above were reviewed with the patient, who was advised to return promptly for any new, worsening or recurrent chest pain, shortness of breath or fainting, and to follow up with their primary care physician in the next few days.
